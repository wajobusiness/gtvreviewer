## 2. THE MANDATORY CRITERION (MC)

### Exact Wording:
- **Exceptional Talent:** The applicant must demonstrate that they have been recognised as a **leading talent** in the digital technology sector within the last 5 years.
- **Exceptional Promise:** The applicant must demonstrate that they have been recognised as having the **potential to be a leading talent** in the digital technology sector within the last 5 years.

### What Counts as Evidence (Examples of Acceptable Evidence in Guidance):
An applicant must provide evidence demonstrating national or international recognition as a leader (Talent) or emerging leader (Promise). Tech Nation guidance specifies four primary categories under MC:
1. **Awards & Recognition:** Proof of national or international awards of merit or recognition for excellence in digital technology.
   - *Guidance requirement:* Must be prestigious, competitive, and independently judged. The applicant must show the judging process, selection criteria, total nominations, and proof of receipt.
2. **Leadership & Scaling in a Product-Led Digital Technology Company:** Evidence of leading the growth, substantial technical innovation, or commercial scaling of a product-led tech company.
   - *Guidance requirement:* Documented proof of personal role and metrics (e.g., revenue growth, active users, commercial contracts, platform telemetry).
3. **High Remuneration / Salary:** Evidence of commanding a high salary or other significant remuneration package.
   - *Guidance requirement:* Remuneration alone is **not sufficient**. It must be supported by official employment contracts, audited tax filings, official payslips, and localized benchmarking demonstrating the applicant is in the top percentiles of tech earners for that market.
4. **Sector Influence & Media Recognition:** Features in reputable national or international media, speaking appearances on the main stage at leading conferences (100+ attendees), or major open-source repositories with substantial industry adoption.

## 3. THE OPTIONAL CRITERIA (OC1 – OC4)

An applicant must select and satisfy **at least two (2)** of the following four Optional Criteria:

### Optional Criterion 1: Innovation (OC1)
- **Exact Wording:** A proven track record for innovation as a founder, senior executive, or employee in a product-led digital technology company or to a new digital technology field/concept.
- **Acceptable Evidence Examples:**
  - Proof of founding an innovative product-led company showing customer traction, investment, or revenue.
  - Granted patents (with application number and Google Patents URL) naming the applicant as inventor. (Patent applications alone are explicitly rejected).
  - Code architecture, technical designs, or proprietary algorithms developed for novel tech products.
  - Independent press coverage validating the product's novel technical concept.

### Optional Criterion 2: Recognition for Work Outside Immediate Occupation (OC2)
- **Exact Wording:** Proof of recognition for work published or contributed outside the applicant's immediate daily occupation that has contributed to the advancement of the digital technology sector.
- **Acceptable Evidence Examples:**
  - Contributions to open-source software projects (e.g., active GitHub repos, merged pull requests, stars, forks, and real-world dependency usage).
  - Authorship of published books, academic papers, or thought-leadership technical articles in recognized industry publications.
  - Mentoring within recognized, structured, selective external programs (primarily offline/in-person; internal workplace mentoring or generic online mentoring platforms like ADPList are explicitly ruled insufficient).
  - Keynote speaking or panel roles at sector-leading technology events with over 100 attendees, where participation was not sponsored or paid for by the employer.

### Optional Criterion 3: Significant Commercial, Technical, or Entrepreneurial Impact (OC3)
- **Exact Wording:** Proof of significant technical, commercial, or entrepreneurial contributions to the digital technology sector as a founder, senior executive, or key employee of a product-led digital technology company.
- **Acceptable Evidence Examples:**
  - Tangible proof of commercial growth: sales pipeline reports, customer growth metrics, enterprise contracts signed, VC investment rounds led.
  - Technical milestones: system architecture diagrams, infrastructure scaling logs, product releases directly authored or architected by the applicant.
  - Independent verification: audited financial accounts, investor updates, third-party platform dashboard exports (Google Analytics, Cloudflare, App Store, Stripe).

### Optional Criterion 4: Academic Research Contributions (OC4)
- **Exact Wording:** Exceptional contributions to the field through academic research, endorsed by a research supervisor or peer-reviewed literature.
- **Acceptable Evidence Examples:**
  - First-author or major-contributor peer-reviewed journal papers and conference proceedings (e.g., IEEE, ACM).
  - Citation counts and h-index metrics demonstrating field-wide impact.
  - Competitive research grants awarded by recognized national or international funding bodies.

## 4. CORE EVIDENCE RULES

1. **Document Count Limit:** Maximum of **10 distinct pieces of supporting evidence** across the entire application (covering the Mandatory Criterion and the 2 chosen Optional Criteria).
2. **Page Limit Per Evidence Piece:** Each piece of evidence must not exceed **3 sides of A4 paper** (maximum 3 pages). Assessors will not review pages beyond the 3-page limit.
3. **Documents Siting Outside the 10-Piece Limit:**
   - The Applicant’s Curriculum Vitae (CV) — maximum **2 sides of A4**.
   - Exactly **3 Letters of Recommendation** — maximum **3 sides of A4 each**.
   - The Personal Statement — completed directly in the Home Office portal (maximum **1,000 words** / 7,000 characters).
4. **Time Window:** All evidence must demonstrate achievements and activity within the **last 5 years** prior to application. Older evidence is deemed irrelevant.
5. **Rule on Distinct Evidence:** Each criterion relied upon must have **at least two (2) distinct pieces of evidence**. Evidence cannot be superficially duplicated across criteria without showing distinct aspects of achievement.
6. **Rule Against Evidence Created Solely for the Visa:** Tech Nation explicitly rejects "evidence created solely to support the application." Articles published days before submission, awards created on demand, or certificates timed specifically to pad the submission are flagged as manufactured and refused.

---

## 5. RECOMMENDATION LETTER RULES

1. **Number of Letters:** Exactly **three (3) letters of recommendation** from three separate individuals.
2. **Author Qualifications:**
   - Must be written by an **established expert** in the digital technology sector.
   - Must hold a senior executive position (e.g., CEO, CTO, Managing Director, Founder, Venture Capital Partner).
   - Must be from a **product-led digital technology organisation** (not an outsourcing, recruitment, or IT support agency).
   - Referees must represent three distinct organisations.
3. **Knowledge of Applicant:**
   - The referee must have direct, detailed knowledge of the applicant’s work over a period of **at least 12 months**.
   - Letters from immediate colleagues, subordinate employees, or personal friends are rejected as lacking independence.
4. **Mandatory Letter Contents:**
   - How the referee knows the applicant and their relationship.
   - The applicant's specific, exceptional achievements in digital technology.
   - How the applicant is considered a leader or emerging leader in the field.
   - How the applicant's presence will benefit the UK digital technology economy.
   - Must be on official organizational letterhead, dated, signed, and provide direct contact details (work email, phone, LinkedIn).
   - Must be accompanied by the author's CV / credentials proving their industry standing.

## 6. DEFINITIONS: PRODUCT-LED VS. CONSULTING & TECHNICAL VS. BUSINESS

### Definition of Product-Led Digital Technology Company:
- A company that designs, develops, owns, and sells its own proprietary software, digital platforms, or digital hardware products (e.g., SaaS platforms, fintech apps, developer tools).
- **Explicit Exclusions:** Companies providing bespoke consulting, outsourced software development, IT maintenance/support, SEO/digital marketing agencies, hardware reselling, or web design agencies.

### Technical vs. Business Applicant Distinction:
- **Technical Applicants:** Software engineers, data scientists, AI researchers, cybersecurity architects, DevOps engineers, systems architects. Evaluated primarily on code contributions, system architecture, patents, and technical depth.
- **Business Applicants:** C-suite executives, founders, commercial directors, product managers, VC investors. Must prove significant commercial leadership in a product-led company (revenue, user acquisition, market expansion, product vision).

## 7. AI USE, REFERENCE CHECKING, AND FRAUD POLICIES

1. **AI Use Rules:** Tech Nation and Home Office policy warns that applications heavily reliant on generic, machine-generated text in personal statements or recommendation letters are penalized. Templated, formulaic, or synthetic text triggers severe assessor skepticism regarding authenticity.
2. **Independent Verification & Fraud Checks:**
   - Caseworkers and Tech Nation assessors routinely independently verify companies, awards, domains, referee identities, and news publications via public databases (Companies House, CAC Nigeria, LinkedIn, Wayback Machine, Google Search).
   - If an assessor encounters fabricated, forged, altered, or fraudulently attributed documents (e.g., misattributed award certificates, tampered revenue dashboards, or ghostwritten letters without genuine executive knowledge), the application will be refused under **Immigration Rules Part 9 (Grounds for Refusal - False Representations / Deception)**, carrying a potential **10-year UK entry ban**.

## 8. EXCEPTIONAL TALENT VS. EXCEPTIONAL PROMISE

| Parameter | Exceptional Talent (Leader) | Exceptional Promise (Potential Leader) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Career Stage | Well-established; typically 5+ years of digital tech experience. | Early career; typically 5 years or less in digital technology. |
| Leadership Standard | Must prove *already recognized as a leading talent*. | Must prove *potential to become a leading talent*. |
| Settlement (ILR) Qualifying Period | 3 years in the UK. | 5 years in the UK. |
| Evidence Weight | Sustained track record, significant commercial/sector-wide milestones. | High-trajectory achievements, early innovative contributions, growth potential. |

## 9. CONFLICTS AND UNCERTAINTIES (OFFICIAL VS. SECONDARY)

1. **Submitting Remuneration as Mandatory Evidence:**
   - *Official Tech Nation Guidance (Notion Guide, updated 2026):* High salary is listed under examples for the Mandatory Criterion as a supporting indicator of market recognition, but guidance explicitly states salary alone is never sufficient.
   - *Practitioner / Forum Reports (EP Advisory & TN Forum):* Assessors frequently reject applicants who submit salary as their primary evidence of leadership if it is from the applicant's own closely-held company without audited independent corporate accounts and tax filings proving real third-party financial scale.
2. **Format of the Personal Statement:**
   - *Immigration Rules / Gov.uk Portal (Since August 2025):* The personal statement is entered as free text (up to 1,000 words) inside the digital Home Office endorsement form, rather than being uploaded as an independent PDF document.
   - *Legacy Guides (Pre-2024 blogs):* Often instruct applicants to upload a 2-page PDF personal statement. The newer official standard is the online form text.
3. **Fast-Track Endorsement:**
   - *Previous Rules:* Certain accelerator alumni (e.g., Techstars, Y Combinator) qualified for fast-tracking.
   - *Current Standard (2025–2026):* Fast track has been phased out under standard Tech Nation digital submissions; all applications follow standard 5-to-8 week review timelines.
